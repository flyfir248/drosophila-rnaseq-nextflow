# Mitochondrial mapping investigation

Sample: SRR1576457
Reference: Drosophila melanogaster FlyBase r6.69
Tool: Bowtie2 2.5.5
Alignment mode: paired-end, very-sensitive, end-to-end
Threads: 2

## Reference properties

Mitochondrial genome length: 19,524 bp
Mitochondrial GC content: 17.78%

## Alignment results

Total read pairs: 29,910,512
Concordantly aligned exactly once: 1,231,143
Concordantly aligned more than once: 2
Discordant pairs: 4,120
Overall alignment rate: 4.14%

Concordant paired-end alignment rate: approximately 4.12%.

## Interpretation

The mitochondrial genome is AT-rich and has a GC content
consistent with the low-GC secondary component observed
in the FastQC per-sequence GC distribution.

The alignment results support a mitochondrial contribution
to this component, but do not establish that all low-GC
reads are mitochondrial.

Mitochondrial reads were retained. No read trimming or
deduplication was performed based solely on these observations.
